Common questions

Things people ask us.

Is VeriWrk free for workers?

Yes. Creating a VeriWrk profile and getting verified is free for individual workers. We believe access to opportunity shouldn't cost you money. Our business model is employer side, not worker side.

What documents do I need to get verified?

You'll need at least one valid government-issued ID. Your NIN or BVN works perfectly. We also accept valid driver's licences and international passports.

How does skills verification work?

Depending on your trade or profession, we use a combination of short practical assessments, work sample reviews, and verified references from people you've worked with.

Who can see my profile?

You control your profile. By default it's visible to verified employers on the platform. You can adjust your visibility settings at any time and you'll always be notified before any employer views or contacts you.

I'm an employer. How do I access verified candidates?

Employers can register for early access now. Once onboarded, you'll be able to search our verified talent pool by role, skills, location, and availability.

Is my data safe with VeriWrk?

Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption and never sell or share your personal data with third parties without your explicit consent. Your VeriWrk profile is yours.

When does VeriWrk launch?

We're in pre-launch now and actively onboarding early access members. We'll be rolling out city by city, starting with Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt.

How is VeriWrk different from a regular jobs board?

Traditional jobs boards list candidates but don't verify them. VeriWrk is built on verification first. Every profile has been identity-checked and skills-assessed. That trust layer is what makes VeriWrk different.
